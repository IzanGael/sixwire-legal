Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Sixwire Publisher is a private tool run by the Sixwire fan project (contact: sixwire.ops@gmail.com). It publishes our own short videos to our own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. Sixwire is a fan project and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

What we collect

When the account owner connects a TikTok account, we receive the basic profile information TikTok shares (account name, open ID, profile picture) and an access token. We also store the videos and captions we choose to publish. We do not collect information about viewers, followers or other TikTok users.

How we use it

Only to publish and schedule our own posts and to read our own post statistics. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

On a private server controlled by the Sixwire project. Access tokens are stored only for the connected accounts and are not shown publicly.

Retention and deletion

We keep the token while the account stays connected. Disconnecting the account in the tool, or removing Sixwire Publisher under TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions), stops access. To have stored data deleted, email sixwire.ops@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
